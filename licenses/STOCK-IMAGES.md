## Usage notes

- Images were chosen to support a medieval herbalist/storybook atmosphere and should be presented cohesively with the site design.
- No unlicensed images, random downloads, or artwork from other games were used.
- CC BY 4.0 image (`alchemist-laboratory-wyck.jpg`) requires attribution if used in published materials; retain this record and provide visible attribution if required by the final publication context.
- All files are stored locally in the project; Unity project assets were not modified.
